## Tile authors

The CDDA-Tilesets repository is a community project with many contributors.

https://github.com/I-am-Erk/CDDA-Tilesets (see per-tile folders for attribution).

and drawn by Yar, edited by I-am-Erk (per `gfx/UltimateCataclysm/tileset.txt` in
the upstream repo).
